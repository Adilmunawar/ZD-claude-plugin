---
name: postmortem
disable-model-invocation: true
argument-hint: "<incident-doc>"
description: Blameless postmortem from an incident doc with action items, owners and dates.
---

Sections: **Summary** (3 sentences) · **Impact** (users, duration, data) · **Timeline** (UTC + local, from detection to resolution) · **Root cause** (the mechanism, not a person) · **Contributing factors** · **Detection** (how, how long it took, how it should have been detected) · **What went well** · **Action items** (table: action, owner, due date, type: prevent/detect/mitigate). Every action item becomes an issue. Under 800 words.
